Maps menu bookmark

One bookmark that sends a restaurant's menu to the Date Nite importer from your own browser. It works on a restaurant's Google Maps page, on its own website, and on ordering sites such as Uber Eats.

Install (once)

  1. Show the bookmarks bar: Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+B (Mac).
  2. Drag the blue button above onto the bookmarks bar.

On Google Maps

Needs you to be signed in to Google, since Maps hides the Menu tab otherwise.

  1. Open the restaurant's page in Google Maps.
  2. Click Menu → Date Nite on the bookmarks bar. It opens the Menu tab, reads each section and opens a filled-in GitHub issue form in a new tab.
  3. Check the menu text and press Create. A venue already on the map is updated; a new one is added.

Breakfast, lunch, brunch, kids and catering sections are skipped.

On the restaurant's website or an ordering site

For menus the importer could not read, and for prices the Maps menu does not have.

  1. Open the page that shows the menu with its prices (the menu page itself, or the site's online-ordering page).
  2. Click Menu → Date Nite. A small panel lists the sections it found, with a count of prices in each. Hover over a section to see its first dishes.
  3. Tick the sections to send. Check the restaurant, town and state, and say whether the venue is already on the map or new.
  4. Press Open the form, then Create on the form.
  • Menu spread over several pages (dinner, dessert, drinks): press Keep ticked, add another page, open the next page of the same site, and click the bookmark again. The kept sections come along, so one form holds them all.
  • Venue already on the map: only the kinds of price the ticked sections cover are changed; the rest keep their saved values.
  • Uber Eats, Postmates, DoorDash, Grubhub: only drinks and desserts are ticked to start, because delivery prices for the mains can run higher than in-store prices.
  • A form needs at least 6 prices between the ticked sections.

The bookmark cannot read a menu that is a PDF, a picture, or shown inside another site's frame. For those, copy the menu text into the Edit a Venue form by hand, or use the site's online-ordering page.

Nothing is sent until you press Create. If a site changes its layout the bookmark may stop finding dishes and will say so.
